## Unsupervised Learning

*No labels. No loss function. Just thinking out loud.*

This is a blog by **Jason Albert** — dad, AI thinker, and occasional philosopher.

Three things I can't stop thinking about:

- **Fatherhood** — the original unsupervised learning problem. No training data. Constant distribution shift. Somehow it works.
- **Artificial Intelligence** — what we're actually building, what it reveals about us, and why the hard questions aren't technical.
- **Philosophy** — the old tools that still cut. Stoicism, mortality, meaning, the examined life.

These three don't always connect neatly. Sometimes they connect in ways I didn't expect. That's what this blog is for.

---

*Unsupervised Learning* is a term from machine learning: a system that finds structure in data without labeled examples. It feels like the right metaphor for how we actually grow — not by following instructions, but by paying attention.
