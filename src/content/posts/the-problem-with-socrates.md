There is no training dataset for fatherhood.

I've read the books. I've scrolled the threads. I've absorbed the unsolicited advice of every stranger in a grocery store who noticed the infant carrier on my chest and felt compelled to tell me it gets *easier* — a statement I've since learned is both true and completely meaningless.

None of it prepared me for the specific texture of 3 a.m. The silence that is never actually silent. The way a small human can look at you with total, catastrophic trust and make you feel simultaneously invincible and wildly underqualified.
